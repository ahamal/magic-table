# Markers

ArUco markers that let the hub find the game board and the gantry head in the camera images, plus a plate to hold the head marker. Generated by [`markers.py`](markers.py) and [`marker_mount.py`](marker_mount.py); change the constants at the top and re-run.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `markers.pdf` | US Letter sheet: board markers IDs 0–3 (40 mm black, 52 mm cut), head marker ID 10 ×2 (34 mm black, 44 mm cut), 100 mm check ruler |
| `marker-mount.stl` | Head marker plate, 48 × 74 × 4 mm, two M5 holes 61 mm apart. Print one. |

```bash
pip install manifold3d numpy opencv-contrib-python-headless pillow
python markers.py
python marker_mount.py
```

## Printing the markers

- Print at **100% / actual size**, not "fit to page". Check the ruler measures 100 mm.
- Matte paper. Cut along the grey lines: the white border is part of the marker.
- Glue fully flat (glue stick or spray adhesive). No glossy tape over them; glare hides them.

## Board markers (IDs 0–3)

- One near each corner of the game board, outside where cards go, all visible to cam 1. Front = the side cam 1 looks from.
- Exact positions don't matter: auto-calibration with the head marker works out the geometry. They only must stay put.
- On a dark board the white border is what makes them findable; don't trim it.

## Head plate (ID 10)

- Bolts flat along a 20×20 extrusion on the head with two M5 bolts into T-nuts (holes 6 mm, sized for this printer). The extrusion backs it along its length; two bolts keep it from turning.
- The 44 mm marker drops between the four corner guides; glue it down.
- Put it on the face cam 1 sees best, on a part that moves with the cup.
- Print flat, guides up. No supports. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls.
