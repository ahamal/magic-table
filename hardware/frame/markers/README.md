# Markers

ArUco markers that let the hub find the game board, the gantry head and Mate in the camera images, plus a plate to hold the head marker. Generated by [`markers.py`](markers.py) and [`marker_mount.py`](marker_mount.py); change the constants at the top and re-run.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `markers.pdf` | US Letter sheet: board markers IDs 0–3 (30 mm black, 40 mm cut), head marker ID 10 ×2 (25 mm black, 33 mm cut), Mate marker ID 20 (30 mm black, 40 mm cut plus an arrow tab), 100 mm check ruler |
| `marker-mount.stl` | Head marker plate, 38 × 64 × 4 mm, two M5 holes 51 mm apart. Print one. |

```bash
pip install manifold3d numpy opencv-contrib-python-headless pillow
python markers.py
python marker_mount.py
```

## Printing the markers

- Print at **100% / actual size**, not "fit to page". Check the ruler measures 100 mm.
- Matte paper. Cut along the grey lines: the white border is part of the marker.
- Glue fully flat (glue stick or spray adhesive). No glossy tape over them; glare hides them.

## Sizes

Tested on the black board (Nov 1): cam 1 found all four 40 mm corners, the far ones at about 50 px across, and still found the far ones when the photo was shrunk to simulate 15 mm markers, to within about 1–1.5 mm. 30 mm corners and a 25 mm head marker keep a margin for dimmer light and other angles. Cam 0 is too low to read any of them; it's for watching picks.

## Board markers (IDs 0–3)

- One near each corner of the game board, outside where cards go, all visible to cam 1. Any ID in any corner.
- **Black board on every side of each marker.** A white border that touches white cloth or paper merges with it, and the marker drops out first.
- Exact positions don't matter: auto-calibration with the head marker works out the geometry. They only must stay put.
- On a dark board the white border is what makes them findable; don't trim it.

## Mate (ID 20)

- Flat and level on Mate's top, facing up, with the arrow tab toward Mate's face (the OLED side). The marker's top edge then gives Mate's heading as well as its position. Trim the tab off after gluing if you like; keep the white border.
- Mate's own camera stays free for peeking at cards and looking at players. Its OLED is too small (about 11 mm square) and faces sideways, so it can't stand in for this marker.

## Head plate (ID 10)

- Bolts flat along a 20×20 extrusion on the head with two M5 bolts into T-nuts (holes 6 mm, sized for this printer). The extrusion backs it along its length; two bolts keep it from turning.
- The 33 mm marker drops between the four corner guides; glue it down.
- Put it on the face cam 1 sees best, on a part that moves with the cup.
- Print flat, guides up. No supports. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls.
