# Mate chassis corners

Printed corners for a box of 4 mm plywood. Panels slide into slots — no drilling, no screws. Generated by [`corner.py`](corner.py); change the constants at the top and re-run.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `corner-normal-v3.stl` | 24 mm corner, 2.5 mm outside walls, 4.5 mm inside walls. Three slots: two walls and the base (or top). One part fits all 8 corners. |
| `corner-thick-v3.stl` | Same, 25 mm with 3.5 mm outside and 5.5 mm inside walls. Stronger slots. |
| `slot-test.stl` | Five slots at 3.9 / 4.1 / 4.3 / 4.5 / 4.7 mm (1–5 notches). Print first. |

```bash
pip install manifold3d numpy
python corner.py
```

## Fit

- **Slot is 4.4 mm** (`SLOT`) for the 4 mm ply; this printer prints tight.
- "4 mm" ply is often 3.6–3.8 mm. Push a scrap into each slot of the slot test, count the notches on the one that holds snugly, set `SLOT` to that width and re-run.
- Each panel goes 15 mm into its slot. A drop of glue makes it permanent; without glue the top can come off.

## Cutting the panels

The panels never touch each other, so every panel is a plain rectangle:

**panel size = box outer size − 18 mm** (normal) or **− 20 mm** (thick), on each side. Cut ~0.5 mm under for slack. Use 8 of the same corner on one box.

Example, a 140 × 90 × 55 mm box (outer):

| Panel | Count | Normal | Thick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base and top | 2 | 122 × 72 mm | 120 × 70 mm |
| Side walls | 2 | 122 × 37 mm | 120 × 35 mm |
| Front and back | 2 | 72 × 37 mm | 70 × 35 mm |

## Printing

- Print with one outer face on the bed. No supports: the slot facing up bridges about 15 mm.
- PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 30–40% infill.

## Notes

- The outer corner is rounded (4 mm) and every other outside edge has a 1 mm round (`ROUND`, `EDGE`).
- The panels are held at the corners only. Between corners there is a 2.1 mm gap along every edge of the box (`BRIDGE`). That plastic is what joins the inside and outside of each slot, so it can't go to zero with plain rectangular panels.
- The wheels sit outside the side walls, so the motor shafts need a way through the wall. That's not designed yet.
